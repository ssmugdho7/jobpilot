Features

Everything you need to land your next role

Built specifically for CSE job seekers in Bangladesh — from your first interview to your final offer.

Live aggregation

Continuous scans of Bdjobs, LinkedIn and 20+ Bangladeshi company career pages — new openings appear automatically, filtered to office/onsite roles.

Smart relevance scoring

Every job is scored against your profile skills and flagged a "Perfect Match" when it fits — so you always know where to focus first.

Skill gap analysis

See which skills each job expects and which ones you're missing — a live checklist to close the gap between you and the role.

Deadline & follow-up radar

Urgency badges when deadlines approach and reminders to follow up 5 days after you apply — you'll never let an opportunity slip.

Company insights

One-click research — Glassdoor reviews and Deshimula company pages open right from each job card so you can vet employers before you apply.

Experience-aware

Fresher, 2-yr, 3-yr and 3+ labels on every job. Filter by what fits your experience level, not a wall of irrelevant postings.

Learning hub

A built-in study companion that rotates through REST APIs, Django, Git, Docker and Linux — upskill while you job-hunt.

Private personal board

Track every job with New / Applied / Dismissed statuses. Your board, your data — protected by secure accounts and invite-only signups.
